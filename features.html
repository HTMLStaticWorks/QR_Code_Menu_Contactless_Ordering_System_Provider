Kitchen Operations

Kitchen Display System (KDS) & Waiter Paging

Eliminate paper tickets that get lost, soaked in sauce, or delayed. Fire tickets directly to dedicated station monitors — Grill, Pantry, Fryer, or Bar — with color-coded preparation timers and audio chime notifications.

  • Multi-Station Ticket Splitting Drinks automatically route to the Bar screen while Steaks route to the Hot Line without human intervention.
  • Course Pacing & Holds Kitchen expediter can hold entrees until appetizers are marked "Served", preventing table clutter and cold food.
  • Runner Paging & Guest SMS Notification When a ticket is bumped to "Ready", runners get a haptic watch vibration, or guests receive an SMS when picking up at the counter.
