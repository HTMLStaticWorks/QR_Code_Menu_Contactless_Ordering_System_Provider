How QRServe Works — From Setup to Table Ordering

No technical knowledge or special app downloads required. Launch your contactless dining system in six simple, automated steps.

01

Create Your Digital Menu

Build your interactive menu with our visual builder. Add categories, high-resolution food photos, prices, dietary tags (Vegan, Halal, Kosher, GF), and customizable modifiers.

Drag-and-Drop Builder
02

Generate Branded QR Codes

Generate high-resolution dynamic QR codes unique to each table, bar stool, cabana, or hotel room. Style with your brand colors and logo, then export print-ready PDFs or order acrylic standees.

Custom Brand Styling
03

Customers Scan With Phone

Diners point their smartphone camera at the table QR code. No app download or account creation needed—the digital menu opens instantly in their native mobile browser in under 1 second.

Works on iOS & Android
04

Browse & Customize Orders

Guests explore appetite-stimulating photos, filter by allergens, choose meat cooking temperatures, add extra toppings, pick sides, and leave specific kitchen preparation notes.

Allergen & Dietary Filters
05

Place Orders Directly to Kitchen

Guests tap "Order Now" and tickets fire straight to your Kitchen Display System (KDS) or POS printer with exact table number and order timestamps—completely eliminating wait times for staff.

Real-Time KDS Fire
06

Seamless Online Payment & Tips

Diners settle bills on their phone with Apple Pay, Google Pay, or credit card, split bills with dining companions, select gratuity percentages, and get digital receipts via SMS or email.

1-Tap Mobile Checkout
