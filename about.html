Origin Story

Born From a 45-Minute Wait in Rome

In the summer of 2021, our founders were dining at a legendary trattoria in Trastevere, Rome. The pasta was extraordinary, the wine sublime. But after finishing dessert, it took 45 minutes to catch the server’s eye, request the check, wait for the POS terminal, and process payment.

The server wasn’t neglectful—he was simply running frantically across 18 tables. We realized that payment and ordering friction is the single greatest bottleneck in hospitality. We set out to build QRServe: not to replace the human magic of dining, but to automate the administrative friction so staff can focus on pure hospitality.

What Drives Us

Our Core Principles

The guiding tenets behind every line of code and hardware design we produce.

Hospitality First

We design for people. Tech should elevate the host, bartender, and runner, never make guests feel like they are in a sterile kiosk.

Relentless Speed

Every millisecond saved in loading dishes or processing a card payment translates directly into higher turnover and happier diners.

Eco Sustainability

Eliminating printed single-use paper menus and plastic laminates saves thousands of trees and millions of gallons of water yearly.

Ironclad Security

PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance, end-to-end tokenization, and strict GDPR privacy safeguards ensure diner data is never sold.
